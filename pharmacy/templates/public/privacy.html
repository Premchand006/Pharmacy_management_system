Privacy notice

This is a template. The pharmacy should review it, fill in the contact details and check it against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules before relying on it.

What we collect

Your name, phone number and address, the prescriptions you show us, and what you buy. Age and gender are optional.

Why

  • To dispense medicines safely and keep the prescription records the Drugs Rules, 1945 require.
  • To issue GST invoices and keep tax records as the GST law requires.
  • To let you see your prescriptions and bills in this portal.

We do not use your data for marketing and do not sell it.

How long we keep it

Invoices are kept for at least six years and prescription registers for at least three, because the law requires it. You can ask us to close your portal account at any time. We then erase your profile, but must keep the copies of your details that appear on past invoices and registers until the legal period ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, or complain, by contacting {{ pharmacy.name }}{% if pharmacy.email %} at {{ pharmacy.email }}{% endif %}{% if pharmacy.phone %} or {{ pharmacy.phone }}{% endif %}. We reply within 90 days. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Access is limited to pharmacy staff with their own logins, every change is logged, and connections are encrypted.
